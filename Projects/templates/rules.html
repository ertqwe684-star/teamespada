RULES AND REGULATIONS

BATTLE ROYALE

  • No one must use hack, panel or script during the tournament. If anyone uses it, him / his team will be disqualified.
  • If any player of a team goes offline during the match, no excuse will be given and the team has to play 3 vs 4 only.
  • For squad only 4 members per team are allowed and for duo only 2 members per team are allowed. No substitute players are allowed.
  • Gun Attributes are turned to be off. Character Skills are allowed.
  • Three rounds will be conducted which contains three different maps: Bermuda, Purgatory and Kalahari.
  • Players must download all the three maps before the tournament starts.

  • No one must use hack, panel or script during the tournament. If anyone uses it, him / his team will be disqualified.
  • If any player of a team goes offline during the match, no excuse will be given and the team has to play 3 vs 4 only.
